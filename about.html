OUR STORY

Where French Savoir-Faire Meets Unhurried Grace

VÉLENI began in a small Paris workshop with one belief: a garment should be cut carefully, finished by hand, and worn for decades. Today our Paris and Milan ateliers make every piece to that standard.

We work with natural fibres, traceable mills, and artisans who are paid fairly for their craft.



WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Craft Principles

01
Made by hand

Every seam and hem is finished by a named artisan in our ateliers.

02
Natural materials

Cashmere, silk, wool and full-grain leather, traced back to the source.

03
Built to last

Repairs, alterations and resoling are offered for the life of the piece.
